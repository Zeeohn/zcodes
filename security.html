Security

How ZCODES apps protect your data.

Architecture

  • Runs on Atlassian. Apps are built on Atlassian Forge and are eligible for the Runs on Atlassian programme: compute and storage are hosted by Atlassian, and the apps make no outbound network calls.
  • Inbound webhooks only. Data enters through Forge web triggers with static responses, so nothing from your site is sent back to the caller.
  • Verified senders. Sentry and CircleCI webhooks are verified with HMAC-SHA256 using constant-time comparison; other senders need a 48-character random token. Invalid requests are rejected before any processing.
  • Secrets. Client secrets, signing tokens and webhook tokens are stored in Forge's encrypted secret storage. Client secrets and signing tokens are never shown again after saving; the generic webhook token is shown only to Jira administrators, inside its URL, and can be rotated at any time.
  • Least privilege. Settings actions check that the caller is a Jira administrator. Scopes are limited to what each feature needs.
  • Licensing. Apps stop processing when their subscription is inactive.

Reporting a vulnerability

Email support@behumbot.xyz with the subject "Security". We acknowledge within 2 business days and keep you updated until it is fixed. Please don't publicly disclose issues before we have released a fix.
